FAQ

Things people ask us first.

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us a WhatsApp message — we usually reply within the hour.

How long until my warung is live with QR ordering?

Most of our customers go live within 7 days of signing on. The actual setup work — building your digital menu, configuring QR codes, training staff — takes about 3–4 business days. We schedule a launch day with you so it lands on a quiet weekday.

What do I need to prepare?

Your current menu (a photo of it is fine), your business name & logo if you have one, and 30 minutes for the kickoff call. That's it.

Can you do it faster than 7 days?

For most cafés and warungs with under 30 menu items, yes — we've done it in 3 days. Tell us when you need to be live and we'll work back from there.

What does the monthly fee actually cover?

Your subscription includes: the QR ordering platform, your digital menu, kitchen tickets, real-time sales dashboard, free menu updates, and WhatsApp support during business hours. Setup is free. Printed QR codes are included on first deploy.

Do I need new hardware?

In most cases, no. A tablet or smartphone in the kitchen is enough to start. If you want a thermal receipt printer, we'll recommend models we've tested and help you set it up.

Does it work offline?

Orders queue locally if the internet drops and sync when it's back. The customer ordering screen needs to be online, but kitchen-side has full offline fallback.

Can customers pay through the app?

Yes. We support QRIS, GoPay, OVO, DANA, ShopeePay, and bank transfer out of the box. You can also keep cash-at-table — many of our customers use a mix.

Is there a contract or commitment?

No long contracts. Pay month to month, cancel anytime. We earn your business by being useful, not by locking you in.
